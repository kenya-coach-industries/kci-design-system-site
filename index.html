Brand

Two marks, used with care.

KCI is the company mark. ISUZU appears alongside it because KCI is an ISUZU dealer.

Do

  • Use official files only
  • KCI left, ISUZU right on documents
  • Clear space: the height of the "K"

Don't

  • Recolour, stretch or add effects
  • Put the red mark on red
  • Redraw the ISUZU logo
Colour

KCI Red is taken from the official logo. Click any colour to copy its token.

Always

  • Codes are 6 digits, single use and expire in 5 minutes
  • Lock sign-in for 15 minutes after 5 wrong tries, and alert IT
  • Say "username or password is incorrect" without saying which
  • Accept pasted codes and the phone's own code suggestion

Never

  • Ask anyone to read out a code by phone or chat; KCI staff never will
  • Send passwords by SMS or email
  • Skip the code step for "trusted" devices longer than 30 days
  • Use national ID numbers as passwords

Trust and anti-forgery

Make fakes easy to spot.

Anyone can edit a PDF or copy a logo. What a forger cannot copy is KCI's own record. Every document points back to it.

1 2 3 4 5 6
protections on every system-generated document
1A system number, never reused

Issued by the system in sequence. A number that does not exist, or belongs to another customer, gives a fake away.

2Status and validity printed by the system

"Approved" and the expiry date only appear once they are true in the system. Drafts print with DRAFT across the page.

3QR code to KCI's own record

Scanning opens verify.kci.co.ke, which shows the customer, total and status from the database. If the paper and the page differ, the paper is fake.

4Short verification code

For people who cannot scan. Generated from the document's contents with a secret key, so it cannot be guessed or reused.

5Digital signature on the PDF

Signed with KCI's certificate. Any edit breaks the signature, and PDF readers show the warning. This replaces scanned handwritten signatures, which are easy to copy.

6Official marks and a security border

Logos and patterned edges deter casual copying, but are never relied on alone: they can be imitated.

Step 1Scan or visit

The customer, a bank or a director scans the QR code or opens verify.kci.co.ke and types the code.

Step 2See KCI's record

The page shows document number, customer, total, status and date, straight from the system. Nothing is read from the paper.

Step 3Compare

Everything matches: genuine. Anything differs, or the code is unknown: treat as forged and call KCI.

Always

  • Generate documents only from the system, as PDF
  • Log every document created, sent and downloaded
  • Send email from kci.co.ke with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set, so spoofed mail is flagged
  • Keep KRA's own eTIMS QR code and control number on tax invoices

Never

  • Send editable Word or Excel quotations
  • Print scanned signature images on documents
  • Put bank details in an email body; point to the portal instead
  • Rely on a logo or watermark as proof

Writing

Say it the way a colleague would.

KCI's own words, short sentences, and always the next step.

Write

  • "Quote sent to Example Transport Ltd."
  • "Check your connection and try again."
  • Quote, claim, price guideline, sales rep

Avoid

  • "Error 3XT"
  • "Invalid input"
  • Labels like "Cust_nm"
Accessibility

Usable by everyone, on any device.

Readable contrast, full keyboard use, large touch targets and meaning that never depends on colour alone.

Every text colour meets WCAG 2.1 AA. Each swatch above says whether it can be used for text.

Keyboard

Everything works with Tab and Enter, with a clear red focus ring. Try it on this page.

Touch

Buttons and fields are at least 44 px tall, easy to tap on a phone.

Not colour alone

Status always has words and a shape, as in the badges and messages above.
